# Awesome Farsi

See [All Language Resources](https://www.alllanguageresources.com/resources/).

- [Finglish Persian phrasebook](https://learnfinglish.com/tools/phrasebook): 400+ everyday words and phrases in English letters, with English meanings and audio; no signup.
- [Finglish English to Farsi translator](https://learnfinglish.com/tools/english-to-farsi-with-pronunciation): gives the Persian script, the pronunciation in English letters and audio; free with a daily limit.
- [Finglish Persian text to speech](https://learnfinglish.com/tools/persian-audio): reads Persian aloud in 8 natural voices; free with a daily limit.
- 💰 [Finglish](https://learnfinglish.com/): spoken Persian lessons in English letters with audio on every word; Module 1 is free with no signup.
