# Awesome Korean

> A curated list of resources for learning Korean.

## Web Apps

- [Tubelang](https://tubelang.com) — Search Korean expressions and see them used in real YouTube clips by native speakers. Free, no signup.
- [Mirinae](https://mirinae.io) — Korean sentence analyzer that breaks down grammar and vocabulary.

## Courses & Structured Learning

- [Talk to Me in Korean](https://talktomeinkorean.com) — Comprehensive grammar lessons from beginner to advanced.
- [KoreanClass101](https://www.koreanclass101.com) — Audio/video lessons with structured learning paths.
- [Coursera: First Step Korean](https://www.coursera.org/learn/learn-korean) — Free university-level course from Yonsei University.
- [How to Study Korean](https://www.howtostudykorean.com) — Free, comprehensive grammar lessons organized by difficulty.

## YouTube Channels

- [Go Billy Korean](https://www.youtube.com/@GoBillyKorean) — Natural-speed Korean explanations in real settings.
- [Korean Unnie](https://www.youtube.com/@KoreanUnnie) — Practical expressions and cultural context.

## Dictionaries & Reference

- [Naver Dictionary](https://dict.naver.com) — The most comprehensive Korean-English dictionary.
- [Papago](https://papago.naver.com) — Korean translation with natural phrasing.

## Flashcards & Spaced Repetition

- [Anki](https://apps.ankiweb.net) — Spaced repetition with community-made Korean decks.
- [Memrise](https://www.memrise.com) — Vocabulary learning with native speaker clips.

## Reading Practice

- [Naver Webtoon](https://comic.naver.com) — Korean webtoons for reading practice.

## Expression & Context Learning

- [Tubelang Expression Guides](https://tubelang.com/korean-expressions/en) — Guides to common Korean expressions with real video examples showing native usage in context.
