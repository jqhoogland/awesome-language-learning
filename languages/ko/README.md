# Awesome Korean

A starter list of resources for learning Korean (한국어). See also [Awesome Korean](https://github.com/mariabnd/awesome-korean) and [All Language Resources](https://www.alllanguageresources.com/resources/korean).

Contributions welcome — please file a PR with any resource you've personally found useful.

## 🎓 Courses & Curricula

- [Talk to Me in Korean](https://talktomeinkorean.com) — The de facto gold standard for Korean grammar. Free at the lower levels (1–9), paid PDFs and workbooks available.
- [How to Study Korean](https://www.howtostudykorean.com) — Free, comprehensive grammar reference from Unit 0 (Hangul) through advanced syntax. Long-form lesson format.
- [Korean TokTok](https://koreantoktok.com) — Free 4-stage curriculum (Hangul → TOPIK 6) with multilingual UI (en/id/vi/de) and free interactive tools (Korean keyboard, Hangul romanizer, [TOPIK score calculator](https://koreantoktok.com/en/tools/topik-score-calculator), Sino/Native [number converter](https://koreantoktok.com/en/tools/korean-number-converter)). No signup. Companion long-form posts: [Hangul roadmap](https://koreantoktok.substack.com/p/learn-hangul-in-1-hour-the-practical), [TOPIK score breakdown](https://koreantoktok.substack.com/p/topik-score-breakdown-what-each-level), [4-stage timeline](https://koreantoktok.substack.com/p/how-long-does-it-take-to-learn-korean), [Sino vs Native numbers](https://koreantoktok.substack.com/p/sino-vs-native-korean-numbers-when).
- [King Sejong Institute (Nuri-King Sejong)](https://nuri.iksi.or.kr) — Korean government-sponsored online courses + textbooks, available in many UI languages.
- [Coursera: First Step Korean (Yonsei University)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/learn-korean) — Free MOOC for absolute beginners.

## 📚 Dictionaries

- [Naver Dictionary](https://dict.naver.com) — The most comprehensive Korean–English dictionary, with example sentences pulled from native sources.
- [National Institute of Korean Language Dictionary](https://krdict.korean.go.kr) — Official Korean dictionary; multilingual definitions (10+ languages).
- [Papago](https://papago.naver.com) — Korean-optimized translation, generally better than Google Translate for Korean.
- [Forvo (Korean)](https://forvo.com/languages/ko/) — Native-speaker pronunciation database.

## 📥 Input

- [SNU LEI: Hangeul](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/snu-lei-hangeul/id1127133132) — Seoul National University's free app for practicing Hangul listening comprehension.
- [Anki + Korean decks](https://ankiweb.net/shared/decks/korean) — Spaced repetition with community-maintained Korean decks.
- [Memrise](https://www.memrise.com) — Vocabulary practice with native-speaker audio clips.

## 🎥 YouTube

- [Talk to Me in Korean (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/@talktomeinkorean) — Grammar explanations + cultural context.
- [Go Billy Korean](https://www.youtube.com/@GoBillyKorean) — Natural-speed grammar explanations, bare-bones production but extremely thorough.
- [Conversational Korean](https://www.youtube.com/@ConversationalKorean) — Real native conversations for listening practice.

## 🎧 Podcasts

- [TTMIK Podcast](https://talktomeinkorean.com/audio) — Multi-level conversational Korean podcast.
- [TTMIK Iyagi](https://talktomeinkorean.com/curriculum/iyagi-series/) — Native-speed conversations (intermediate+).

## 📝 Reading

- [Naver Webtoon](https://comic.naver.com) — Korean webtoons, casual writing style — great low-effort reading practice.
- [Namu Wiki](https://namu.wiki) — Korean Wikipedia alternative; more casual writing style than Wikipedia ko.

## 💱 Exchange

- [HelloTalk](https://www.hellotalk.com) — Language exchange with native Korean speakers.
- [Tandem](https://www.tandem.net) — Same idea; somewhat smaller Korean community than HelloTalk.

## 🎯 TOPIK

- [TOPIK Guide](https://www.topikguide.com) — Test preparation, mock papers, and official-style explanations.
- [Korean TokTok TOPIK score calculator](https://koreantoktok.com/en/tools/topik-score-calculator) — Predict your TOPIK level from raw section scores; useful for tracking improvement across mock papers.

## 🔠 Hangul (writing system)

- [Korean TokTok Hangul roadmap](https://koreantoktok.substack.com/p/learn-hangul-in-1-hour-the-practical) — One-hour practical roadmap to read Hangul.
- [Wikipedia: Hangul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hangul) — Background on the script's history and structure.
