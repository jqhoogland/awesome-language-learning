# Awesome Nederlands (Dutch)

See also [All Language Resources](https://www.alllanguageresources.com/resources/dutch).

## 🎓 Courses & apps
- [Dutch Daily](https://dutchdaily.app) - App built around the Dutch integration exams (inburgering/NT2) with AI voice conversation scenarios. The [de/het trainer](https://dutchdaily.app/de-or-het/), sentence structure trainer and KNM practice exam are free without an account.
- [Nedbox](https://www.nedbox.be/) - Free interactive exercises by KU Leuven.
- [NT2 TaalMenu](https://nt2taalmenu.nl/) - Free exercises for all levels: reading, writing, grammar.

## 📚 Grammar & dictionaries
- [dutchgrammar.com](http://www.dutchgrammar.com/) - The reference grammar site for Dutch learners.
- [Woordenlijst](https://woordenlijst.org/) - The official Dutch word list, with conjugation tables.
- [Wiktionary NL](https://nl.wiktionary.org/) - Dutch dictionary with definitions and translations.

## 🔊 Listening & pronunciation
- [Forvo Dutch](https://forvo.com/languages/nl/) - Native speaker pronunciations for single words.
- [Een Beetje Nederlands](https://www.eenbeetjenederlands.nl/) - Podcast in slow Dutch with free transcripts.
- [NOS Journaal in Makkelijke Taal](https://npo.nl/start/serie/nos-journaal-in-makkelijke-taal) - Daily news broadcast in simple Dutch.
